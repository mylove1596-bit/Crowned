Support

Getting help

If something is not working, or you have a question about your purchase, we would like to hear from you.

Email: support@crowned.app
Or open an issue on GitHub: mylove1596-bit/Crowned

Please include:

  • the model of your iPhone and the iOS version (Settings → General → About)
  • what you did, what you expected to happen, and what happened instead
  • a screenshot if the problem is visual

We answer support email within a few business days.

Frequently asked

How do I restore my purchase? Open the app, tap the Pro button on the home screen, then tap "Restore purchases" at the bottom of the paywall. Your Pro access returns immediately.

I bought Pro on another device. Use the same "Restore purchases" button. The purchase is tied to your Apple ID, not to your device.

How do I cancel a subscription? Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us. On your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → choose the subscription → Cancel Subscription. You keep access until the end of the period you already paid for.

Do I need an internet connection? No. Every puzzle is generated on your device. The app only needs a connection to confirm a purchase or to refresh your Pro access.

How does the daily puzzle work? Every player gets the same puzzle on the same day, because the puzzle is generated from the date on your own device. A new day starts at 00:00 in UTC+8. When the app says the daily challenge is done, it means you have already completed today's puzzle.

Why did my streak reset? A streak counts consecutive days on which you completed the daily puzzle. If you missed a day, the streak goes back to 1 — unless you have a streak-repair shield, which can be used from the streak banner on the home screen to keep the streak alive.

How many hints do I get? One smart hint per day for free, and the allowance resets at the start of each new day (00:00 UTC+8). Pro includes unlimited hints.

Why is the 8×8 level locked? It unlocks for free once you have solved five puzzles. The 9×9 difficulty is part of Pro.

Does the app record anything? No. See the privacy policy for the details. Nothing about your session is uploaded.

How do I place a crown? Tap a cell to cycle it through empty → × → crown. Drag across cells to paint a row of × at once. When you place a crown, the app marks the cells it rules out automatically — you can switch that off in Settings.

Reporting a problem

We are glad to hear about bugs. If you find one, please email support@crowned.app with the steps to reproduce it. We will look into it.

If you believe you have found a security issue, please email support@crowned.app with "security" in the subject line rather than opening a public issue.
