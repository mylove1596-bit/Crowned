2. What the app is

Crowned is a single-player logic puzzle. Each puzzle is a square grid of cells divided into coloured regions. You place one crown in every row, so that no two crowns share a row, a column, a region or touch each other diagonally. Every puzzle has exactly one solution.

4. Daily challenge

Every day, Crowned publishes one daily puzzle. It is generated on your device from a fixed seed derived from the date, so every player gets the same puzzle on the same day and it does not require an internet connection.

A new day starts at 00:00 in UTC+8. Your streak, your daily hint allowance and the daily challenge reset at that moment.

5. Hints and streaks

  • Free players receive one smart hint per day. Pro players receive unlimited hints.
  • Your streak counts consecutive days on which you completed a daily puzzle.
  • If you miss a day, a streak-repair shield can be used to keep the streak alive.
  • Shield balances and streak records are stored on your device.
