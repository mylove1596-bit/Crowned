Same puzzle. Same day.

Someone shared a Crowned puzzle with you. Crowned is a daily logic grid: place one crown in every row, every column and every coloured region — and never let two crowns touch, not even diagonally.

Every day at midnight your time zone, everyone gets the same puzzle. This link points at one exact puzzle, so you can play exactly what they played.

How to play

  • One crown per row.
  • One crown per column.
  • One crown per coloured region.
  • No two crowns may touch — not side by side, not diagonally.

Tap a cell and it cycles empty → × → 👑. Drag to mark several × at once.
