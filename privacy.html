Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

The short version: Crowned collects nothing. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you do stays on your device.

1. Who we are

Crowned is published by an independent developer. Questions about this policy can be sent to mylove1596@gamail.com.

2. What we collect

Nothing.

Crowned has no server of its own. There is no sign-up, no email address, no name, no advertising identifier (IDFA), and no third-party analytics or advertising SDK. The app does not ask for the App Tracking Transparency prompt, because it does not track you across other apps or websites.

3. What stays on your device

The following is stored locally using the system preferences facility (UserDefaults), and is never transmitted anywhere:

DataWhy
Your current and best streak, and the date of your last completed daily puzzleTo show your streak and to decide whether a new day has started
Your remaining daily smart hints and the date they were last resetTo limit free hints to one per day
Whether automatic × marking and haptic feedback are onYour gameplay preferences
Your unlock status for the paid featuresTo show which parts of the app are available to you
Your solved puzzles and your best timesTo show your archive and statistics

4. Puzzles are generated on your device

Every puzzle, including the daily challenge, is generated locally from a fixed seed. The daily puzzle is derived from the date, which is why everyone gets the same puzzle on the same day. No puzzle data is requested from or sent to a server.

5. The Home Screen widget

The optional streak widget and the app share data through an Apple App Group. This is a private container that belongs to Crowned alone. The shared data never leaves your device and is not readable by other apps.

6. Purchases

Payments are handled entirely by Apple. We never see or store your card details, your billing address or your Apple ID. Apple may share limited transaction information with us (for example, a purchase token and a subscription status) so that we can unlock the features you paid for. You can manage or cancel a subscription at any time in your device's Settings.

7. Children

Crowned is not directed at children under 13, and because it offers purchases it is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, at any age, so there is no information to delete on request.

8. Your rights

Because we do not collect or store personal information on our servers, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct, export or delete. To remove everything the app holds, delete the app from your device — this clears its local storage along with it.

9. Third parties

Crowned uses no third-party SDKs, no advertising networks, no crash-reporting services and no social logins. The only third party involved is Apple, which delivers the app and handles payments.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published here with a new date at the top. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
