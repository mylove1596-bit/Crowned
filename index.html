Crowned

The daily logic puzzle that takes five minutes.
One puzzle a day, changing at your local midnight. Works completely offline.

Get Crowned on the App Store

How to play

  • One crown per rowNo two crowns may share a row, a column, a colour region, or touch each other — even diagonally.
  • Tap or dragTap a cell to cycle empty → × → crown, or drag across cells to paint them quickly.
  • Exactly one answerEvery puzzle is generated so that it has a single solution. No guessing, no guessing twice.

Why it's different

  • No ads. No account. No sign-up.
  • Works on a plane, on a train, or in a queue with no signal.
  • Every puzzle is generated on your phone from a date-based seed — everyone in the same time zone gets the same puzzle on the same day, without a server.
  • Keep a streak going, and come back tomorrow for a new one.
